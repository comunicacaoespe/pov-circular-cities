Privacy and cookies

Last updated: November 2026

About this website

This website presents the Erasmus+ KA220-YOU project “POV: Circular Cities” and is managed by the project partnership.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, and it does not load content from third parties such as maps or videos. Because of this, no cookie banner is needed.

What is saved in your browser

When you choose a language, the site saves that choice in your browser’s local storage (item “pov-lang”) so the page opens in the same language next time. It is needed only for the site to work as you asked, it never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Technical data when you visit

Like any website, our hosting provider’s servers automatically process technical data needed to deliver the pages and keep them secure, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request and the browser type. We do not use this data to identify visitors and we do not combine it with any other information. Fonts and images are served from this website, so no data is sent to font or image services.

Personal data we do not collect

The site has no accounts, forms, comments or newsletter, so we do not ask for or store names, e-mail addresses or any other personal details. If this changes, this page will be updated before the new feature goes live.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to partner organisations and to the Creative Commons licence. Those websites have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we do not control.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact the project partnership.

Changes to this page

If the website changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding analytics or a contact form, we will update this page and the date above.
